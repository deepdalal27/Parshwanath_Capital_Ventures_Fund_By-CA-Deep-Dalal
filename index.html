01Position

Committed domestic capital, deployed with a research-first discipline.

The Fund partners with issuers, lead managers and intermediaries to put committed capital to work across primary and secondary market opportunities.

Parshwanath Capital Ventures Fund is a SEBI-registered Category III Alternative Investment Fund managed by Parshwanath Fund Managers LLP. It operates a flexible, multi-strategy mandate across the Indian capital markets, combining rigorous fundamental analysis with disciplined valuation and a long-term ownership mindset.

That mandate lets us meet an opportunity wherever it appears — as an anchor investor in a mainboard or SME issue, as a long-term holder of a listed business, or as a participant in a rights issue, buyback or open offer where an event has created a mispricing.

What does not change is the standard applied before capital moves. We are sector-agnostic but not indifferent: we look for scalable businesses, clean governance and credible promoter track records, and we are willing to say no when any of the three is missing.

02Strategy

One mandate, four ways to express it.

A flexible multi-strategy approach, sector-agnostic by design and selective by discipline.

01

Primary markets

Anchor investor participation in mainboard and SME IPOs, pre-IPO placements and qualified institutional placements of quality issuers. We evaluate issues on business fundamentals and valuation, not on listing-day expectations.

02

Listed equities

High-conviction positions in listed companies held with an ownership mindset, with the flexibility for long and short exposure permitted to a Category III AIF where the risk-reward justifies it.

03

Special situations

Rights issues, buybacks, open offers and other event-driven opportunities across market capitalisations, where a corporate action rather than a change in the business creates the opportunity.

04

Selection standard

Sector-agnostic, with a stated preference for scalable businesses, clean governance and credible promoter track records. The standard is the same whichever strategy an opportunity arrives through.

Every investment is an ownership stake, not a trade. Positions are underwritten on durable fundamentals, margin of safety and patient capital rather than short-term market noise — including positions entered through a primary issue.
The full investment process
03Process

How an opportunity becomes a position.

A swift internal evaluation cycle, but never a shortened one. Every stage has an explicit reason to stop.

01

Origination

Opportunities reach us from issuers, lead managers and intermediaries, and from our own screening of the listed universe and corporate-action calendar. We are willing to look early — at DRHP and pre-filing stages, under NDA where required.

02

Fundamental analysis

Financial statements, cash conversion, capital allocation history and the durability of the business are examined before any view is taken on price. Accounting quality and related-party behaviour are treated as gating items, not footnotes.

03

Governance & promoter review

We assess the promoter's track record, the quality of disclosure and the history of how minority shareholders have been treated. A business we like on numbers is still declined when this review fails.

04

Valuation & margin of safety

Intrinsic value is assessed as a range under conservative assumptions across sectors and market capitalisations. In a primary issue, that assessment is made independently of the price band before the band is considered.

05

Decision & sizing

Size follows conviction and downside rather than enthusiasm, and reflects the liquidity and any lock-in attaching to the instrument. Our internal decision cycle is deliberately short so that issuers receive a clear answer quickly.

06

Ownership & review

Positions are held with a long-term orientation and reviewed against the thesis that justified them. We exit on a broken thesis or on valuation that no longer offers a margin of safety — not on price movement alone.

Why the Category III structure

Category III is the AIF category for funds employing diverse or complex strategies, including investment in listed and unlisted derivatives. It is the structure that permits the breadth this mandate requires.

  • Anchor participation in primary issues alongside listed-market investing in a single vehicle
  • Long and short exposure where the risk-reward justifies it
  • Freedom to hold cash when the opportunity set is unattractive
  • Position sizing driven by conviction rather than index weights

The permissions a Category III AIF enjoys can be used aggressively. Ours is a long-biased mandate, and the flexibility exists to manage risk rather than to manufacture returns.
