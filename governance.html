Governance & Compliance

Independent oversight, written policies, and a regulator to answer to.

The safeguards between an investment manager's judgement and an investor's capital — and the mechanisms available to you if we fall short of them.

01Framework

The regulatory framework we operate within.

Registration is a licence to operate, not a seal of approval. It is worth being precise about what it does and does not mean.

Parshwanath Capital Ventures Fund is registered with the Securities and Exchange Board of India as a Category III Alternative Investment Fund under the SEBI (Alternative Investment Funds) Regulations, 2012, bearing registration number IN/AIF3/25-26/2108. The Fund, its investment manager Parshwanath Fund Managers LLP and its trustee Axis Trustee Services Limited are bound by those regulations and by the circulars and reporting obligations SEBI issues under them.

That framework governs how the Fund may raise capital, who may invest, how conflicts of interest must be managed, what must be disclosed to investors and to the regulator, how portfolio securities must be held and valued, and how investor grievances must be handled. Participation in primary issues is additionally subject to the SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, including the rules governing anchor investor allocations and lock-ins.

It is important to state plainly what registration does not mean. SEBI has neither approved nor endorsed the Fund, has not passed any opinion on the merits of the strategy, and does not guarantee the accuracy of any statement made by the Fund or its manager. Registration does not protect investors against loss.

02Separation

Nobody should have to take our word for it.

Every function that could be abused by an investment manager sits with an independent third party.

01

Independent trustee

Axis Trustee Services Limited acts as trustee to the Fund, holding its assets on behalf of investors and overseeing the manager's compliance with the fund documents.

02

Independent custodian

Portfolio securities are held with a SEBI-registered custodian. The investment manager can direct trades but never takes possession of investor assets or cash.

03

Independent valuation

Net asset value is computed by an appointed fund accountant, independent of the manager, in accordance with the valuation policy set out in the fund documents.

04

Independent audit

The Fund's financial statements are audited annually by independent chartered accountants, and the audited accounts are made available to investors.

03Policies

The policies that bind us.

Adopted formally, applied to every decision, and available to investors on request.

The manager maintains a written policy identifying situations in which its interests, or those of its personnel, could conflict with those of investors. Personal trading by the manager and its personnel is restricted and monitored; no personal account may transact ahead of the Fund in a security the Fund is dealing in, including in any issue in which the Fund has indicated anchor interest. Where a conflict cannot be avoided, it is disclosed to the trustee and to investors.

Concentration limits, liquidity constraints, lock-in exposure and governance exclusions are documented before capital is deployed and monitored on an ongoing basis. Breaches — including passive breaches caused by market movement — trigger a documented review and, where required, corrective action within a defined period. The policy also governs the circumstances in which derivatives and short exposure may be used.

Listed securities are valued at market prices from recognised exchanges in accordance with the methodology stated in the fund documents. Instruments subject to lock-in or not yet listed are valued in accordance with the policy's prescribed approach for such holdings. Valuation is performed by the independent fund accountant, not by the investment manager.

All investors are subject to know-your-customer verification and screening in accordance with the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 and SEBI's master directions before units are allotted. Source of funds is verified, records are retained for the prescribed period, and the Fund reserves the right to decline any subscription without giving reasons. Investments are accepted only from the investor's own verified bank account.

The manager and its personnel are bound by a code of conduct addressing the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015. This is particularly material to our primary-market work: information received at DRHP or pre-filing stage, or under a non-disclosure agreement, is recorded and results in the relevant security being placed on a restricted list until the information is public or stale. Research is otherwise conducted from public sources and lawful primary research.

The Fund makes the periodic disclosures to investors and to SEBI required under the AIF Regulations, including reporting on portfolio composition, valuation, fees and expenses, and any material change to the fund documents, the investment strategy or the key persons of the manager. Material changes requiring investor consent are not made without following the process prescribed in the fund documents.

04Grievances

If something goes wrong.

A clear, time-bound escalation path — including one that does not run through us.

Step one — write to us

Raise the matter with us in writing at fundmanager@parshwanath.in, including your name, folio or commitment reference and a description of the issue. Every complaint is logged on receipt and acknowledged. We aim to resolve complaints within 30 days, and will tell you if a matter will take longer and why.

Step two — escalate to the trustee

If our response is unsatisfactory, the matter may be escalated to Axis Trustee Services Limited, the trustee of the Fund, whose contact details are set out in the private placement memorandum. The trustee is independent of the investment manager.

Step three — SEBI SCORES

If a complaint remains unresolved you may lodge it with SEBI through the SCORES platform at scores.sebi.gov.in. Investors may also use the Online Dispute Resolution mechanism notified by SEBI, available through the SMART ODR portal, for disputes that remain unresolved after the SCORES process.
