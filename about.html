The Firm

01The firm

Who manages the Fund.

A deliberately flat structure: the person who researches an opportunity is the person who decides on it and the person who answers to you for it.

Parshwanath Capital Ventures Fund is a SEBI-registered Category III Alternative Investment Fund, registration number IN/AIF3/25-26/2108, which commenced in October 2025. The Fund is managed by Parshwanath Fund Managers LLP and its trustee is Axis Trustee Services Limited.

The Fund was established to deploy committed domestic capital across the Indian capital markets with a discipline that is common in private markets and rarer in public ones: form a view on what a business is worth before looking at what is being asked for it, and be willing to decline on governance grounds regardless of the price.

We work directly with issuers, lead managers and intermediaries as well as with investors. Both relationships depend on the same thing — a quick, honest answer, and a reason for it.

What we are. A single-strategy Category III AIF investing across primary issues, listed equities and special situations in the Indian markets.
What we are not. A multi-strategy platform, a quantitative shop, a leveraged trading desk, or a distributor of third-party products.

03Structure

Independent oversight by design.

Everything that is not investment judgement sits with an independent third party.

Investment management is kept in-house and deliberately concentrated. The functions that could be abused by an investment manager — custody of assets, valuation, audit and oversight — are held by parties independent of it.

The manager never takes custody of investor assets or cash. These arrangements address operational and custodial risk; they do not protect against market losses.
