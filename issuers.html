For Issuers & Lead Managers

Committed domestic capital, and a clear answer inside your timetable.

We partner with issuers, lead managers and intermediaries across mainboard and SME issues, pre-IPO placements and qualified institutional placements.

01Proposition

What we bring to the table.

For a merchant banker building an anchor book, two questions matter: can they decide quickly, and will they still be there after listing?

01

Committed domestic institutional capital

A SEBI-registered Category III AIF with committed capital and a swift internal evaluation and decision cycle. You deal directly with the Fund Manager, so questions are answered by the person making the decision.

02

Repeat participation across issues

We aim to be a reliable name on your anchor and investor lists rather than an opportunistic one — a fund you can approach again on the next issue because the last engagement was straightforward.

03

Stable post-listing shareholding

A long-term ownership orientation that supports quality price discovery and a stable shareholding pattern after listing. We are not underwriting a listing-day exit.

04

Willingness to engage early

We will evaluate opportunities at DRHP and pre-filing stages, under non-disclosure agreement where required, so that you have an indication of interest well before the anchor book is assembled.

02Scope

Where we participate.

Across the primary market and the corporate-action calendar, sector-agnostic by mandate.

Instruments

  • Anchor investor allocations in mainboard IPOs
  • Anchor and institutional participation in SME issues
  • Pre-IPO placements
  • Qualified institutional placements
  • Rights issues, buybacks and open offers
  • Other event-driven and special-situation opportunities

What we look for

  • Scalable businesses with a credible growth runway
  • Clean governance and consistent, complete disclosure
  • Promoters with a demonstrable track record
  • Financial statements where profit reconciles to cash
  • A price band that leaves a margin of safety against our own valuation

Research depth is currently concentrated in capital market players, gems and jewellery, gold lenders, specialty chemicals and waste management, though the mandate itself is sector-agnostic.

03Engagement

How to approach us.

A short, predictable path from first contact to a firm answer.

01

Introduction

Write to the Fund Manager directly with the issue, the stage it has reached and your indicative timetable. An NDA can be executed before any confidential material is shared.

02

Materials & diligence

We review the DRHP or offer document, financial statements and the promoter and governance record, and revert with focused questions rather than a generic checklist.

03

Management interaction

Where useful, a direct conversation with the management team — on the business and its capital allocation rather than on the marketing of the issue.

04

Indication of interest

We form a view on intrinsic value independently of the price band, then indicate interest and size against it. If we are not interested, you will be told plainly and promptly.

05

Participation

Confirmation and allocation are handled through the standard anchor or placement process, in accordance with applicable SEBI regulations.

Please note. Nothing on this page is a commitment to participate in any issue, an assurance of subscription, or a recommendation in respect of any security. All participation is subject to our own diligence, the terms of the offer document and applicable SEBI regulations.

Bringing an issue to market?

Write to the Fund Manager directly. We will tell you quickly whether it is an issue we want to look at, and we are happy to sign an NDA first.
