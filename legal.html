Investment risk

Investments in Alternative Investment Funds are subject to market and other risks, including the possible loss of the entire principal amount invested. The Fund invests in listed equities and in primary market issues and may therefore experience significant volatility and material drawdowns. Category III AIFs are permitted to employ leverage and to invest in derivatives, which can magnify both gains and losses.

Participation in initial public offerings, pre-IPO placements, qualified institutional placements and special situations carries additional risks, including the risk that allotment is partial or nil, mandatory lock-in periods during which a position cannot be exited, limited public track records for newly listed companies, and reduced liquidity — particularly in SME issues and smaller companies.

Past performance, whether of the Fund, the investment manager or any person associated with them, is not indicative of and does not guarantee future results. Prospective investors must read the complete risk factors set out in the private placement memorandum.

02Privacy

Privacy policy.

What we collect, why we collect it, and what we will never do with it.

What we collect

If you submit an enquiry through this website, we collect the information you choose to provide — typically your name, email address, telephone number, the nature of your enquiry and your message. If you become an investor, the registrar collects the additional information required for know-your-customer verification under applicable law, through its own secure process rather than through this website.

Why we use it

Enquiry information is used solely to respond to you, to assess whether the Fund is suitable for your objectives, and — if you ask to receive them — to send you our investor letters. Investor information is used to administer your investment and to meet our legal, regulatory and reporting obligations.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.
  • We do not share it with marketers, distributors or data brokers.
  • We do not use it for any purpose unrelated to your enquiry or your investment.

We disclose personal information only to our service providers acting on our behalf under confidentiality obligations — such as the trustee, registrar, custodian, fund accountant and auditor — and where disclosure is required by law, by a regulator or by a court.

Retention and security

Personal information is retained for as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected and for the period required by applicable law and regulation, including record-keeping obligations under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002. We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect it. No transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure, which is why we ask you not to send identity or bank documents through this website.

Cookies and analytics

This website is deliberately built without advertising trackers, third-party behavioural profiling or cross-site cookies. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may record the request in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. If any analytics are introduced in future, this policy will be updated to say so before they are enabled.

Your rights

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it where we are not required to retain it, and withdraw consent to receiving our letters at any time. Write to fundmanager@parshwanath.in and we will respond within a reasonable period.
