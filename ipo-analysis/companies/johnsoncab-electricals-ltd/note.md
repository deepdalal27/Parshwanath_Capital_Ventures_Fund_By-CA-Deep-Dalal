## 1. Business of the company

- **What it does:** manufactures **low-voltage wires and cables up to 1.1 kV** under the "Johnson Cables" brand. The range covers power, control, house/building wires, solar, submersible flat and welding cables.
- **Plants:** two manufacturing units in **Mehsana, Gujarat**, with combined installed capacity of **79,950 km a year** (FY26). Utilisation is not available without the RHP.
- **Customers:** a mix of private companies, government entities and distributors/dealers. The split and concentration are not available.
- **Promoters:** Chandreshkumar Shankerlal Patel, Anjal Chandreshkumar Patel and Bhavanaben Chandreshkumar Patel.

## 2. Industry outlook

- **Demand:** low-voltage cables go into housing, commercial buildings, industry, agriculture pumps and solar installations. Housing, infrastructure spending and rooftop/utility solar are steady drivers.
- **Structure:** the market is fragmented. National brands (Polycab, KEI, Finolex, RR Kabel, Havells) lead the organised segment, with many regional manufacturers below them. Small players compete mainly on price, dealer reach and government tenders.
- **Economics:** copper and aluminium are most of the cost and largely passed through, so margins are thin (high single digits to low teens) and working capital is heavy, especially when selling to government customers.
- **Read for an investor:** a growing market with room for regional players, but returns depend on working-capital discipline and the copper cycle rather than pricing power.

## 3. Moat and SWOT analysis

### Moat

- **Product breadth and approvals:** a wide LV range lets the company serve dealers and project customers from one source.
- **Regional cost base:** Gujarat manufacturing close to western-India demand.
- **Limits:** no structural barrier against larger brands. The edge is execution and relationships, which the RHP would let us test.

### SWOT

**Strengths**
- Revenue CAGR 54% FY24-26 to Rs 210.47 cr
- ROE 42.7% and ROCE 39.7% in FY26
- Wide LV product range including solar and submersible cables
- 79,950 km a year installed across two plants

**Weaknesses**
- Debt/equity 2.54x before the issue
- Working capital absorbs most of the profit (cash flow statement not available)
- Small scale against national brands
- Both plants in one district

**Opportunities**
- Housing, infrastructure and solar demand
- Paper-insulated copper and aluminium conductors for transformer makers (new line funded by the issue)
- Rooftop solar cuts power cost

**Threats**
- Copper and aluminium price swings
- Price competition, organised and unorganised
- Delayed payments from government customers

## 9. Conclusion

**Neutral at Rs 200 (upper band).**

**The investment case.** A fast-growing Gujarat maker of low-voltage cables. Revenue has grown 2.4x in two years to Rs 210 cr, EBITDA margin has risen to 11.4%, and ROE is above 40%. The issue is all fresh money, for capacity, a new conductor line and working capital, and it roughly halves leverage. At 15.75x FY26 earnings it is priced below listed SME cable peers (median 20.2x), and the reported P/E is a fair measure on the data available.

**What holds the value down.** Growth has been funded by borrowings and working capital, so free cash flow has been negative. FY26 is the first year at an 11% margin, and leverage before the issue is high (2.54x).

**Valuation.** Fair value Rs 141 / **Rs 176** / Rs 215 (Bear / Neutral / Bull).

**Call rule.** The Neutral value is 11.7% below the upper band (between 10% and 30%), and the Bull value is above the price, so the call is **Neutral**. At the Rs 190 floor it would read Subscribe. No material issue was found in the data available.
