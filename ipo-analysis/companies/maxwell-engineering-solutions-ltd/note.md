## 1. Business of the company

- **What it does:** manufactures **aftermarket components for underwater pelletizing and extrusion systems**, the machines polymer plants use to cut molten plastic into pellets. Products include die plates, cutting blades, blade holders and driving bushes. It also refurbishes and re-engineers this equipment (company website).
- **Plant:** one factory at Waghodia GIDC, Vadodara, Gujarat, with CNC, VMC, EDM, wire-cut and furnace equipment.
- **Customers:** polymer and petrochemical producers. The company says it supplies clients in more than 50 countries. Customer concentration and the export share are not available without the RHP.
- **History:** incorporated on 13-Sep-2022 as a private company and since converted to a public company. The promoters say they have more than two decades of collective experience with underwater pelletizing systems. Promoter names, and whether an earlier business was transferred into the company, are not available without the RHP.

## 2. Industry outlook

- **Demand:** every polymer plant that runs underwater pelletizers needs die plates, blades and holders replaced regularly. Demand follows the installed base and plant utilisation rather than new capacity alone, so it is more recurring than the capex cycle.
- **Structure:** the original equipment makers (mostly European and US) dominate spares. Independent aftermarket suppliers compete on price, lead time and refurbishment.
- **Read for an investor:** a real niche with recurring demand and room for a low-cost Indian supplier. The risks are that it is small, technical and exposed to OEMs defending their spare-parts margins.

## 3. Moat and SWOT analysis

### Moat

- **Technical know-how:** die plates and blades are precision, wear-critical parts, and the promoters' experience with pelletizing systems is the core asset.
- **Cost:** an Indian cost base against OEM spare-parts pricing.
- **Limits:** a young company with one plant. Whether it has qualified with large customers on repeat contracts is not visible without the RHP.

### SWOT

**Strengths**
- Niche aftermarket parts for underwater pelletizers
- FY26 RoNW 47.7%, ROCE 38.5%

**Weaknesses**

- Single plant

**Opportunities**
- Worldwide installed base of pelletizing lines
- Refurbishment and re-engineering services
- New machinery and lab funded by the issue

**Threats**
- OEMs defending their spare-parts business
- Polymer industry downturns lowering plant utilisation
- Customer concentration (not disclosed)

**Read:** neither is a close peer. ### What would change our view

- **Towards Neutral:** a price at or below about Rs 122 (the Bull value), or RHP figures showing the margin dip is seasonal and cash flow tracks profit.
- **Towards Subscribe:** a credible listed peer set supporting a multiple near 20x, together with stable margins.
- **Further towards Avoid:** related-party dependence or customer concentration revealed in the RHP.

### Risks

- Margin compression and price competition from OEMs.
- Customer and end-market (polymer) concentration.
- Short operating history and execution of the expansion.
- SME liquidity and the 1,600-share minimum lot.

## 9. Conclusion

**Avoid at Rs 158 (upper band).**

**The investment case.** A niche Vadodara engineer making wear parts for the pelletizers used in polymer plants worldwide. It has grown fast, earns very high returns (FY26 RoNW 48%), and is raising Rs 58 cr of fresh money to add machinery, a building and working capital.

**What holds the value down.**
- The company is three years old.
- Its 52% FY26 margin has already fallen to 36% in the latest four months, which lifts the P/E from 18x to about 24x.
- There is no usable listed peer, so the value rests on cash flows, and those stay thin while the expansion is funded.

**Valuation.** Fair value Rs 49 / **Rs 84** / Rs 122 (Bear / Neutral / Bull), DCF only.

**Call rule.** The Neutral value is 46.9% below the upper band and the Bull value is also below it, so the call is **Avoid**. This is a valuation call. No governance or accounting issue was found in the data available.

This is a **provisional** report built without the RHP. A better peer set or RHP detail on margins could move it to Neutral.
