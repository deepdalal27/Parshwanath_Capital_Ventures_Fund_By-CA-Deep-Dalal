## 1. Business of the company

- **What it does:** a crop-solutions company founded in 1994 (p. 304). It sells:
  - crop protection products: herbicides, fungicides and insecticides;
  - natural crop solutions: bio-stimulants, plant growth regulators and micronutrients;
  - hybrid seeds for field crops, vegetables and flowers.
- **Revenue mix FY26:** crop protection and natural crop solutions 80.4%; seeds 19.0% (p. 306). In Q1 FY27, the kharif selling season, seeds reached 36.5%.
- **Brands:** 193 product brands (p. 305). The company is among the most active acquirers in Indian agrochemicals. It has bought established brands from multinationals: Gramoxone and Tilt (Syngenta), Bavistin (BASF), Dursban (Dow) and Furadan (FMC) (p. 308).
  - In FY25 it paid **Rs 483.46 cr for brand assets** and **Rs 242.98 cr for the I&B Seeds business** (p. 104).
- **Manufacturing:**
  - Four formulation plants with 75,962 MT a year of capacity: Sonipat, Anand and two in Jammu.
  - Two plants making technicals (active ingredients) with 4,717 MT: Dahej and Nagpur.
  - Seed processing at Hyderabad and Bengaluru.
  - A new plant is planned at Jhagadia, Gujarat (p. 307).
- **Reach:** 14,717 active distributors in 23 states; about 1,200 sales staff (pp. 307-308).

## 2. Industry outlook

Industry figures come from the Frost & Sullivan report commissioned and paid for by the issuer (p. 304). We have not checked them independently.

- **Seeds:** the Indian seed market is about USD 4.3 bn in FY26, growing at about 7.4% a year to FY31 (p. 273).
- **Crop protection:**
  - India's per-hectare use of agrochemicals is low, and farmers are moving to branded, combination and biological products.
  - The company's branded crop-protection business grew 46% over FY24-26, against an industry rate of 10-13% (p. 305).
- **Drivers and swings:**
  - The monsoon and pest pressure drive annual demand.
  - Prices of Chinese technicals drive input costs and channel inventory.
  - Regulatory bans on molecules are a recurring risk.
- **Read for an investor:** a steady-growth industry where brands and distribution reach decide returns. Working capital is heavy because of channel credit and the seasonal build of stock.

## 3. Moat and SWOT analysis

### Moat

- **Brands bought from multinationals** with long farmer recall, plus 18 granted Indian patents (p. 307).
- **Distribution:** 14,717 distributors; over 98% of revenue comes from repeat customers (p. 35).
- **Backward integration:** the company can make or import technicals depending on prices (p. 307).
- **Limits:** generics compete on price, and the acquired brands need continual marketing spend.

### SWOT

**Strengths**
- 193 product brands, including acquired Gramoxone, Bavistin, Tilt and Furadan (pp. 305, 308)
- 14,717 active distributors in 23 states (p. 307)
- Own technicals plants at Dahej and Nagpur (p. 307)
- Top-3 in pearl millet and mustard seeds (p. 306)

**Weaknesses**
- Return on equity about 8% after brand amortisation (p. 186)
- Working capital about 38% of revenue (p. 186)
- Seasonal and monsoon-dependent
- Auditor's modified opinion (p. 448)

**Opportunities**
- Seeds growing at 28% CAGR FY24-26 (p. 306)
- Natural crop solutions and biologicals (p. 305)
- New Jhagadia plant (p. 307)
- Lower interest after the issue

**Threats**
- Weak monsoon or pest cycles
- Price swings in Chinese technicals
- Molecule bans
- Pricing pressure from generics

- **Board:** six directors, three of them independent (p. 415).
  - Ankur Aggarwal is Chairman and Managing Director.
  - Nand Kishore Aggarwal is a promoter and executive director.
- **Ownership:**
  - **Before the offer:** the promoters hold 80.19% on a fully diluted basis (p. 137). Financial investors include:
    - IFC Emerging Asia Fund 5.18%
    - IFC 3.11%
    - WhiteOak 1.03%
    - an employee welfare trust 4.73% (p. 145)
  - **After the offer:** after the fresh issue and the OFS the promoters would hold about 71.5% (our computation).
- **Cost to the selling promoter:** weighted average cost Rs 23.51 a share (p. 1).
- **Audit:** Walker Chandiok & Co LLP issued a **modified opinion** across FY24-Q1 FY27 (p. 448).
  - **The issue:** an insurance claim receivable of Rs 16.30 cr, booked in FY13, was rejected by the insurer. The company won at the consumer commission (NCDRC); the insurer has appealed to the Supreme Court.
  - **The auditor's view:** the receivable is a contingent asset and should not be recognised.
  - **The effect:** equity would be Rs 16.30 cr lower (0.9%), with no effect on FY24-26 profit.
- **Litigation (p. 36):**
  - Rs 255.94 cr of claims against the company, 11.77% of net worth. These include 85 criminal cases, mostly Insecticides Act sampling matters common in agrochemicals.
  - Contingent liabilities of Rs 113.27 cr at Jun-26, mainly entry tax Rs 69.20 cr and GST Rs 33.35 cr (p. 603).
- **Read:** a founder-family business with institutional investors and an independent board. The modified opinion is a balance-sheet matter of under 1% of equity, and the litigation is typical of the industry. Neither is a material issue under the Fund's rules.

### Is the reported P/E right?

**Verdict: the reported FY26 P/E of 30.6x overstates the multiple. On recurring earnings it is 27.1x.**

## 9. Conclusion

**Avoid at Rs 272 (working upper band), by a hair. It reads Neutral at Rs 271 or below and at the Rs 259 floor.** The band is unconfirmed at writing, and the call will be re-checked when it is announced.

**The investment case.**
- A sizeable Indian agrochemicals and seeds company: FY26 revenue Rs 3,152 cr, gross margin 38.5%, adjusted EBITDA margin 15%.
- It has 193 brands, many bought from multinationals, and a 14,700-distributor network.
- The issue repays debt.
- Cash generation over FY24-26 was strong: CFO 2.2x PAT.

**What holds the value down.**
- Amortisation of acquired brands roughly equals profit, so the return on equity is about 8%.
- Working capital is heavy.
- At 30.5x reported (27.1x adjusted) earnings, the price is well above the 19.9x peer median.
- The auditor's modified opinion is small but noted.

**Valuation.** Fair value Rs 157 / **Rs 190** / Rs 225 (Bear / Neutral / Bull).

**Call rule.** At Rs 272 the Neutral value is 30.1% below the upper band and the Bull value is also below it, so the call is **Avoid**. The margin is 0.1 percentage points. No material governance or accounting issue was found.
