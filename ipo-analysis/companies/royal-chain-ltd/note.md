# Royal Chain Limited: DRHP screen

> **Basis.** Draft Red Herring Prospectus dated 28-Sep-2026, filed with SEBI on 01-Oct-2026. Restated standalone financials to 31-Mar-2026, **187 days old** at the date of this note. **No price band yet.** All figures are from the DRHP (page numbers are the printed folios) and are in ₹ million unless stated. Upside is measured against **₹147.33**, the December 2025 pre-IPO placement price adjusted for the 5:1 bonus. That is a reference point, not a price band. Exit route assumed: 2–3 year hold.
>
> **This is a DRHP screen, not a decision.** The RHP must carry financials no more than six months old, so it should include H1 FY27. That half-year is the period that tests the thesis below.

## Verdict: Screen only, leaning *Avoid* at or above ₹147

Reported profits have risen fast: PAT went from ₹273.62 m (FY24) to ₹1,733.42 m (FY26). **None of it has reached the bank.** Cash flow from operations was negative in all three years, at −₹115.67 m, −₹30.49 m and −₹2,165.17 m (p. 66). Inventory nearly doubled to ₹6,958.87 m in FY26 (p. 64). Debt funded the gap, and ₹6,500 m of the ₹8,500 m fresh issue will repay that debt (p. 95). The IPO is in substance a **balance-sheet refinancing of a working-capital build**. At the ₹147.33 reference, the business is priced on earnings it has not yet converted into cash.

## The offer

- **Size:** ₹10,000 m, of which a ₹8,500 m fresh issue and a ₹1,500 m offer for sale. The sellers are Suresh and Manish Jain (₹500 m each) and the promoter firms Celestial Charms and Foxy Feathers (₹250 m each) (p. 61).
- **Eligibility and structure:** Regulation 6(1). QIB portion no more than 50%, non-institutional at least 15%, retail at least 35% (p. 61).
- **Anchor reservation:** of the anchor portion, 33.33% is reserved for domestic mutual funds and 6.67% for life insurers and pension funds (p. 61). On a ₹10,000 m issue the anchor book is up to ₹3,000 m. After those reservations about **₹1,800 m is open** to other anchors, including AIFs. Allocation is discretionary, and anchor shares are locked in, half for 30 days and half for 90 days.
- **Shares:** 125,344,092 pre-issue, after the 5:1 bonus of 08-Sep-2026 (p. 78). Promoters hold 93.19% (p. 82). ESOP 2026 has been adopted but no options have been granted (p. 92).
- **Lead managers:** JM Financial and 360 ONE WAM.

The working-capital build has continued after the audited period. Repaying revolving working-capital lines frees headroom that will be redrawn if inventory keeps growing. Read the objects as a refinancing, not deleveraging.

## Key findings

1. **Profit is not cash.** Cumulative CFO from FY24 to FY26 is −₹2,311.33 m against cumulative PAT of ₹2,640.35 m, a cash conversion of **−87.5%**. Before working-capital changes, operating profit was ₹2,965.23 m in FY26. Working capital then absorbed ₹4,558.20 m (p. 66).
2. **Growth came from gold price and traded stock, not volume.** Revenue doubled from FY24 to FY26 while production *fell* from 4,490.81 kg to 4,004.81 kg (p. 111). FY26 production used about 33% of the 12,000 kg installed capacity. In FY25, purchases of stock-in-trade (₹19,129.94 m) exceeded materials consumed (₹16,081.27 m) (p. 65).
3. **The margin jump coincides with a rising gold price on a doubled inventory.** Gross margin went from 4.62% to 7.84% and EBITDA margin from 2.71% to 6.09% (p. 111). The cash-flow statement carries a ₹78.72 m mark-to-market on unfixed gold price and ₹742.28 m of gold on lease appears for the first time in FY26 (pp. 64, 66). The DRHP does not split margin into a manufacturing component and an inventory holding-gain component. Until it does, treat FY26 margins as **not yet demonstrated to be repeatable**.
4. **Working capital scales badly.** Each rupee of incremental FY26 revenue required **₹0.42** of incremental trade working capital. Trade NWC rose from 12.5% to 19.4% of revenue, and the issuer's own working-capital days went from 39 to 56 (pp. 64, 111).
5. **Related parties sit at the top of the customer list.**
   - Aalishaan Jewels LLP, where key managerial personnel have significant influence, bought ₹2,392.48 m in FY26 (5.06% of revenue) and sold ₹2,072.63 m to the company (4.38%). It was the **top customer** in FY26 (p. 32) and became a subsidiary with a 99% profit share from 01-Apr-2026 (p. 70).
   - Sales to related parties account for about 16% of the revenue growth from FY25 to FY26.
   - The company bought the gold jewellery business of promoter-interested Royal Italian Jewellery Pvt Ltd for ₹466.20 m by slump sale, and in FY26 took a ₹342.50 m unsecured loan from the same entity (p. 70).
6. **Tax is paid in cash, which is the strongest positive fact here.** Current tax was ₹620.00 m on PBT of ₹2,324.04 m (26.7%), and 26.9% cumulatively (p. 65). The profits are taxed as real income.
7. **Auditor churn.** S. Mandanmal Mehta & Co resigned on 16-Jul-2025 because it lacked peer-review credentials. Jain V. & Co was appointed to fill the casual vacancy, and KKC & Associates was added as joint auditor on 30-Jun-2026 (p. 73).
8. **The pre-IPO price sets the reference.** On 02-Dec-2025, LMJF Capital Advisors LLP bought 1,221,720 shares at ₹884 (₹1,080 m), which is ₹147.33 after the bonus. That valued the equity at about ₹18,467 m (p. 79). At that price, after the fresh issue, the trailing P/E on FY26 PAT is about 15.6x, close to the peer median of 15.72x.

## Valuation (₹ per share, post-issue diluted 18.30 crore shares at the ₹147.33 reference)

| Scenario | DCF | Relative | Blended 60:40 | vs ₹147.33 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Bear | 40.49 | 173.90 | 93.85 | −36.3% |
| Neutral | 69.45 | 173.90 | 111.23 | −24.5% |
| Bull | 130.39 | 173.90 | 147.79 | +0.3% |

- **DCF:**
  - WACC is 15.78%: cost of equity 18.20% (risk-free 6.5% + 1.1 × 7.0% + 2% size + 2% company-specific for negative cash conversion and family control) and cost of debt 8.15% (FY26 interest ÷ average borrowings).
  - Terminal growth is 4.50%.
  - Neutral assumes revenue growth easing from 18% to 9%, an EBITDA margin of 4.5% (roughly the three-year average, below FY26's 6.09%) and NWC of 19% of revenue.
  - Terminal value is **81.2% of EV**, so the DCF is mostly a terminal assumption.
- **Relative:** uses the DRHP's own peer set (p. 110): Sky Gold & Diamonds 43.96x, Shringar House of Mangalsutra 15.72x, Shanti Gold International 15.34x P/E. The median P/E of 15.72x gives ₹148.83; the median P/B of 3.92x on post-issue net worth gives ₹281.99. EV/EBITDA can't be computed because the DRHP gives no peer debt or EBITDA. A 10% discount for size and cash conversion is applied.
- **The divergence is the finding.** The DCF is 60% below relative value because relative valuation prices FY26 *reported* earnings, while the DCF prices *cash* after working capital absorbs about 19% of every incremental rupee of revenue. Peer multiples assume earnings convert to cash; this issuer's have not.
- **Reverse DCF:** FY26 free cash flow to the firm was **−₹2,738.8 m**, so no multiple can be stated. **At ₹147.33, the entire price rests on a cash turn that has not yet begun.** Holding growth at 4.5% and WACC at 15.78%, the price needs a steady-state EBIT margin of about **10.4%**, against 5.6% in FY26. No positive working-capital intensity justifies it on its own.
- **The dominant lever is margin, not WACC.** One point of EBITDA margin moves Neutral value by about ₹20.60 per share. One point of WACC moves it by about ₹6–7.

## What would change our view (test against the RHP)

1. **Restated CFO for H1 FY27 is positive**, or cumulative CFO from FY24 to H1 FY27 turns positive.
2. **Closing inventory days on revenue fall below 40** at 30-Sep-2026. They were 53.7 at 31-Mar-2026 and about 36 at 31-Mar-2025.
3. **H1 FY27 EBITDA margin stays at 5.5% or more**, with the RHP disclosing the gold-price holding gain or loss within it.
4. **Price band cap at or below about ₹111**, the Neutral blended value, or a band that prices FY26 PAT at a clear discount to the 15.7x peer median.
5. **Aalishaan consolidation is disclosed with like-for-like revenue**, so growth can be separated from consolidation of a related party.

## Risks

- The gold price can move against an inventory of about 54 days of revenue. A fall would reverse the FY26 margin gain.
- Working-capital debt is repayable on demand. Credit rating and covenants were not reviewed in this pass.
- 93.19% promoter ownership, with a promoter trust and two promoter firms selling in the OFS.
- After listing, the 6-month lock-in on LMJF's 7.33 m shares (5.85% before the issue) expires into a small float.

- **Peer P/E reproduces:** each peer's P/E equals its price divided by its diluted EPS.
