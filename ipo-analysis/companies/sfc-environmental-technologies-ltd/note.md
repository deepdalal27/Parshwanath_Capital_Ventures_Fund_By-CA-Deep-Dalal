## 1. Business of the company

- **What it does:** environmental technology for:
  - **wastewater treatment (WWT):** its proprietary C-Tech sequencing batch reactor (SBR) process plus the key equipment (decanters, diffusers, turbo blowers, fibre-disc filters, solar sludge dryers) and PLC / SCADA automation, then supervision, O&M and spares;
  - **wastewater recycling and reuse (WRR):** DuPont ultrafiltration, CCRO and MBR membranes, under an exclusive Indian distribution agreement;
  - **solid waste treatment (SWT):** integrated municipal-waste plants, now also compressed biogas (CBG).
  (pp. 299-300)
- **Scale:** 705 WWT installations treating 17,144 MLD at Mar-26 (724 / 17,465 MLD at Jun-26). F&S, in an issuer-paid report, puts its share of India's SBR market above 80% (pp. 188, 299).
- **Segments, FY26 (p. 501):**

  | Segment | Revenue | Segment result |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | WWT | Rs 542.76 cr (68%) | Rs 172.67 cr |
  | SWT | Rs 140.16 cr (17%) | Rs 40.13 cr |
  | Automation (Chavare Engineering) | Rs 106.50 cr (13%) | Rs 11.95 cr |
  | WRR (new) | Rs 14.21 cr | Rs 9.22 cr |

- **Customers:** mainly EPC contractors building municipal STPs, plus Goa Waste Management Corporation (GWMC) in SWT. Top customer 14.34% and top-10 64.44% of FY26 revenue (p. 307).
- **New lines:** two CBG plants (Tinsukia 125 TPD, Bhubaneswar 300 TPD) won by subsidiary HWTPL from a public-sector oil company, Rs 501.70 cr including 10-year O&M (p. 300). It also bought 80% of IST-Anlagenbau GmbH (Germany, solar sludge dryers) on 30-Jul-2025 for Rs 32.32 cr (pp. 312, 510).
- **Order book:** Rs 1,574.52 cr at Mar-26 (2.0x FY26 revenue) and Rs 1,570.07 cr at Jun-26, against Rs 560.39 cr a year earlier (pp. 184, 188).

## 2. Industry outlook

Industry figures come from the Frost & Sullivan report commissioned and paid for by the issuer; we have not checked them independently.

- **Sewage treatment:** India's market grew 5.4% a year over FY20-25 and is projected to grow 9.6% a year over FY25-30 (p. 173).
- **Recycling and reuse (tertiary treatment):** Rs 1,870 cr in FY26, projected to reach Rs 7,013 cr by FY31, a 30.3% CAGR (p. 173).
- **Compressed biogas:** F&S estimates CBG production at 1.7 MMTPA in FY26, which could reach 26.5 MMTPA by FY31 under the CBG blending obligation on oil marketing companies (p. 173).
- **Competition:** listed water / bio-energy players Thermax, Praj Industries and Ion Exchange (p. 186), VA Tech Wabag, and many EPC contractors. SFC's position is upstream: the technology supplier the EPC contractor specifies at tender stage.
- **Read for an investor:** demand is policy-led and long-term; the cash cycle follows municipal payment cycles through the EPC contractor.

## 3. Moat and SWOT analysis

### Moat

- **Installed base and specification:** over 80% of India's SBR STP installations (issuer-paid F&S) means consultants and EPC contractors specify C-Tech, and each plant adds spares and O&M (p. 299).
- **Proprietary process plus in-house equipment:** C-Tech and OREX are proprietary; decanters, blowers (Turbomax JV), automation and now solar dryers (IST) are made in-house, so it earns technology and equipment margins together (pp. 299, 312). WWT segment result was 31.8% of segment revenue in FY26 (p. 501).
- **Exclusive DuPont membranes** for municipal WRR in India (p. 299).
- **Operating record in SWT:** an 8-year-old integrated MSW-to-biogas plant in Goa, which supports CBG bids (p. 300).
- **Limits:** contracts are mostly purchase orders, and payment depends on EPC contractors and municipal budgets.

### SWOT

**Strengths**
- Over 80% share of India's SBR STP installations; 705 plants, 17,144 MLD (issuer-paid F&S; pp. 188, 299)
- Operating EBITDA margin 29.3% in FY26 (p. 188)
- Order book Rs 1,574.52 cr, 2.0x FY26 revenue (p. 188)
- WWT revenue back to growth: +15.7% in FY26 (p. 501)
- Profit taxed in cash: current tax 27% of PBT (p. 412)

**Weaknesses**
- Operating cash flow negative in FY26 and Q1 FY27 (p. 417)
- Working capital 76.5% of revenue; cash conversion cycle 242 days (pp. 188, 411)
- Moved to net debt of Rs 38.82 cr at Mar-26 from net cash a year earlier (p. 188)
- Top-10 customers 64.44% of revenue (p. 307)
- Seasonally weak Q1: Q1 FY27 PAT Rs 1.48 cr (p. 412)

**Opportunities**
- Tertiary treatment market projected at 30.3% CAGR FY26-31 (issuer-paid F&S, p. 173)
- CBG orders of Rs 501.70 cr with 10-year O&M (p. 300)
- DuPont membranes for municipal water reuse (p. 299)
- Solar sludge dryers through IST-Anlagenbau (p. 312)

**Threats**
- Ten pending Goa pollution-board / GWMC notices and an FIR at the SWT plants (pp. 554-556)
- EUR 5.60 m (Rs 59.75 cr) liquidated-damages claim in Portugal (p. 556)
- Payment delays from municipalities via EPC contractors (p. 31)
- Technology displacement (p. 28)

- **Why DCF and peers diverge (-71%):** the DCF discounts at 14.3% and charges the business for the working capital its growth needs (about 70 paise per rupee of extra revenue). That caps it near 11x FY26 earnings. Listed water and energy peers trade at 31-52x. The divergence is the finding: the band sits between the two, much closer to the cash-flow view than to peer multiples.
- **Sensitivity (Neutral DCF):**

One lever at a time, it needs perpetual growth of 11.9% (vs 4.5%), or a WACC of 9.9% (vs 14.3%), or a steady-state EBITDA margin of about 47% (vs 28%). **In plain words: the price pays for some franchise value beyond today's cash flows, but stays well below what listed peers command.**

## 9. Conclusion

**Subscribe at Rs 330 (upper band).**

**The investment case.** SFC is the dominant technology supplier for SBR sewage treatment plants in India: 705 installations, a 29% operating margin, a business that grew 15% in FY26 with its core WWT segment recovering, and an order book of twice annual revenue after wins in compressed biogas and water reuse. At Rs 330 it is priced at 20.9x FY26 earnings, below every listed peer. The reported P/E is a fair measure of its earnings.

**What to keep an eye on.** Cash conversion is the weak spot: working capital has risen to 77% of revenue and operating cash flow was negative in FY26 and Q1 FY27. About four-fifths of the offer goes to early shareholders whose average cost is Rs 36.72. The Goa waste plants have collected ten pollution-board and customer notices since Jul-2025.

**Valuation.** Fair value Rs 358 / **Rs 392** / Rs 423 (Bear / Neutral / Bull). **Call rule:** at the upper band the Neutral value is 18.8% above the price, so the call is **Subscribe**. Even the Bear value (+8.4%) is above the price. No material issue was found.
