## 1. Business of the company

- **What it does:** a B2B maker of **flexible packaging** from one plant at **Barasat, West Bengal**: multilayer LD films, shrink films, barrier films, printed laminates, and milk and oil pouches. Lamination was 59% of FY26 revenue.
- **Capacity:** installed capacity rose from 6.85 to **8.85 million kg a year** in FY26, at **71.64% utilisation**. A further extrusion coating and lamination line of 12.85 million kg a year is planned.
- **Trading:** it also trades billet strips on a bill-to-ship basis; this is part of revenue, and the share is not available.
- **Markets:** West Bengal was **96.8%** of FY26 revenue. Trackers flag a sharp rise in counterparty concentration.
- **Promoters:** Pankaj Kumar Maheshwari, Sujata Maheshwari and Sethsawaria Poly Fab LLP; the promoter group holds **98%** before the issue.

**Classification: early scaling.**

## 2. Industry outlook

- **Demand:** flexible packaging serves food, dairy, edible oil and FMCG; demand grows with packaged consumption and the shift from loose to packed goods.
- **Structure:** fragmented, with large national converters and many regional players. Raw material is mainly polyethylene and other polymers, whose prices move with crude; margins are usually high single digits to low teens.
- **Read for an investor:** steady volume demand, but a commodity-linked, competitive business where regional players win on service and proximity rather than pricing power.

## 3. Moat and SWOT analysis

### Moat

- **Proximity and service** to dairy and edible-oil packers in eastern India; a broad range from one site.
- **Limits:** one plant, one state, and no structural barrier against larger converters.

### SWOT

**Strengths**
- Revenue up 2.3x in two years; EBITDA margin 16.7% in FY26
- RoNW 26% in FY26
- Capacity of 8.85 million kg with 28% headroom
- Promoter backing: Rs 15.87 cr of promoter loan converted to equity in FY26

**Weaknesses**
- Operating cash flow negative for three years
- Borrowings up 3.5x to Rs 46.82 cr (debt/equity 1.11x)
- West Bengal 96.8% of revenue; single plant
- Supplier credit collapsed (payables Rs 21.40 cr -> Rs 1.68 cr)

**Opportunities**
- Packaged dairy and edible-oil growth in eastern India
- New extrusion coating and lamination line
- Higher-value barrier films and laminates

**Threats**
- Polymer price swings
- Price competition from larger converters
- Customer concentration

### Risks

- Polymer price volatility.
- Single plant and single-state revenue.
- Working-capital stretch and rising debt.
- Execution of the new line.
- SME liquidity and the 2,400-share minimum application.

## 9. Conclusion

**Neutral at Rs 94 (upper band).**

**The investment case.** A West Bengal flexible-packaging maker that moved from trading into manufacturing. Revenue has grown 2.3x in two years to Rs 115 cr, EBITDA margin has risen to 16.7%, and RoNW is 26%. The promoters have put in Rs 15.87 cr of their own money, and the issue funds working capital, debt repayment and machinery. At 17.3x FY26 earnings after the issue it is priced below the two listed peers' average (21.0x), and the reported P/E is a fair measure on the data available.

**What holds the value down.** The business has not generated operating cash for three years. Borrowings have risen 3.5x, sales are almost entirely in one state from one plant, and FY26 is the first year at this margin.

**Valuation.** Fair value Rs 57 / **Rs 68** / Rs 81 (Bear / Neutral / Bull). **Call rule:** the Neutral value is 27.8% below the upper band (between 10% and 30%), so the call is **Neutral**. It is close to the 30% line, which would apply above about Rs 97. No material issue was found in the data available.
