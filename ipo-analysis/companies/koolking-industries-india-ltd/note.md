# Koolking Industries India Limited: DRHP screen

> **Basis.** Draft Red Herring Prospectus dated 23-Sep-2026 (SEBI filing page dated 01-Oct-2026), 100% book-built fresh issue of Rs 3,000 m plus an offer for sale of up to 3,050,000 shares by four selling shareholders (three promoters and one promoter-group member), under Reg 6(1) of the ICDR. Keynote Financial Services Limited is sole BRLM. Restated financials run to 31-Mar-2026, **176 days before the DRHP date and 198 days old at the date of this note**. No price band exists. All issuer figures are from the DRHP; printed page numbers are cited. Only the merchant-banker section uses outside sources, each named and unverified.
>
> Upside is measured against **Rs 115.82**: FY26 basic EPS of Rs 6.07 times 19.08, the lowest peer P/E in the DRHP's own table (p. 179). It is a reference to size the model, not a price band and not a view of the right price. Exit route assumed: 2-3 year hold.
>
> A DRHP is a screen, not a decision. The RHP must carry financials no more than six months old and should include H1 FY27. Until it is read, this note supports no commitment of capital.

## Verdict: Screen only. The evidence is mixed.

**Strengths:** profit has grown fast, returns are high, and the company is eligible on the profit test.
- PAT rose from Rs 76.70 m (FY24) to Rs 271.37 m (FY25) to Rs 332.06 m (FY26); reported EBITDA margin from 6.52% to 10.89% (p. 182).
- Reported ROCE was 28.39% and ROE 42.86% in FY26 (p. 182); adjusted for foreign exchange, 32.38% and 53.71%.
- Current tax is 25.4% of cumulative PBT, close to the statutory rate (p. 86).
- It supplies eight of the top ten Indian HVAC makers (issuer-commissioned CARE report, p. 174); 96.91% of FY26 revenue was from repeat customers; 10 customers carried out 108 audits in three years (pp. 175, 182).
- Reg 6(1) eligibility reproduces: average operating profit Rs 366.37 m against Rs 150 m required (p. 475).

**Concerns:** growth has slowed, it is not turning into cash, and the exposure is concentrated.
- Revenue was +101.4% in FY25 but +9.0% in FY26 (Rs 5,267.45 m), a year in which copper and brass prices rose (pp. 86, 442).
- Operating cash flow was +Rs 37.40 m, -Rs 66.47 m and -Rs 430.08 m; cumulative CFO is -67.5% of cumulative PAT (p. 87). Inventory days rose to 116 from 50 (p. 167).
- Ten customers are 89.71% of revenue and Amber Enterprises alone 28.79%; Amber is also named among major suppliers. Supplier 1 is 32.99% of expenses (pp. 24-26).
- A customs show-cause notice of Rs 130.38 m (14.2% of net worth) is pending before the Supreme Court; unhedged imports produced a Rs 74.96 m FX loss in FY26 (pp. 444, 461).
- The three new plants (Rs 1,530.00 m) have no orders placed (p. 161). At the Rs 115.82 reference our blended fair value is Rs 59.25 / 76.33 / 92.98 (Bear / Neutral / Bull); the Neutral DCF breaches our terminal-value guard (see Valuation).

The two readings separate on whether the FY26 working-capital build is temporary. If inventory (Rs 1,700.24 m) and receivables (Rs 1,061.51 m) convert to cash as the issuer's plan assumes (inventory days from 116 to 98 by FY29), the returns shown are real. If margins are held only by passing through metal prices and by 90% customer concentration, growth will keep consuming cash. **H1 FY27 cash flow, the customs outcome, the Amber purchases and the RHP price band are what resolve it.**

## Business model

The company makes U-bends, evaporator inlet and outlet tubes and sockets (indoor-unit components), suction, condenser and discharge tubes and C-bends (outdoor-unit components), brass flare and dead nuts, and refrigeration lines, from copper and brass (pp. 174-175, 25). It sells to air-conditioner makers. FY26 revenue was indoor-unit components Rs 2,208.20 m (41.9%), outdoor-unit components Rs 2,111.67 m (40.1%), scrap sales Rs 670.75 m (12.7%) and other lines Rs 276.83 m (5.3%, our residual) (p. 442). Scrap is counted in revenue from operations and rose 51.1% in FY26.

The DRHP does not describe pricing or metal pass-through terms; its MD&A says FY26 cost of materials rose with copper and brass prices (p. 442). Gross margin was 28.91% in FY26 (p. 182). The company relies on third parties for transport and on contract labour (1,257 workers in FY26 against 883) (p. 444). Imports are 53.07% of raw material purchases, from South Korea, China and Thailand, and are not hedged (pp. 21, 444).

## Industry analysis

The industry data come from the CARE Industry Report, **commissioned and paid for by the company** (p. 20), so market-size and growth claims are issuer-sourced. CARE puts the company's addressable market at Rs 34.8 bn in FY26 growing 24.46% a year to FY30, and Indian air-conditioner volume at 12.8 m units in FY27 rising to 16.7 m by FY30 (9.3% CAGR) (pp. 137, 174). On that market the company claims a 15% share (Rs 5,267 m against Rs 34.8 bn). The structure is a supplier tier to a small group of air-conditioner makers (ODMs and OEMs); the listed names the issuer compares itself with are PG Electroplast, Amber Enterprises, KRN Heat Exchanger, Elin Electronics, Centum Electronics and EPack Durables (p. 179), of which Amber and KRN are also its customers. Government localisation policies are cited as supporting domestic sourcing (p. 137). Demand is seasonal (summer cooling), cyclical with consumer durables, and exposed to metal prices.

## Moat

- **Pre-qualification.** ODMs and OEMs approve suppliers through technical audits; the issuer cites this as a barrier (p. 174). It protects an incumbent's seat, not its price, and the dependence runs through ten customers that are 89.71% of revenue.
- **Proximity.** Plants near customer hubs cut logistics cost and lead time (p. 175). A real but replicable advantage; the three new units extend it.
- **Scale and cost.** Installed capacity rose from 279.86 m to 419.26 m units over FY24-FY26 (p. 182). Gross margin of 28.91% and a reported EBITDA margin of 10.89% do not show a pricing premium.
- **Switching costs.** Qualified-supplier status is sticky in the short run (repeat customers 96.91%), but no contract terms, tenor or volume commitments are disclosed.
- **Read.** Durable seat with a few large customers; limited pricing power, and no evidence of a structural cost or brand advantage in the numbers.

## Revenue and profit growth drivers

- **Revenue.** FY25: +101.4% (indoor +67.6%, outdoor +112.5%, scrap +115.9%) on higher volumes, per the MD&A (p. 442). FY26: +9.0% (indoor +12.7%, outdoor +14.5%, scrap +51.1%) with higher copper and brass prices; sales volume is not disclosed, so how much of FY26 growth is price cannot be measured.
- **Profit.** PBT grew 18.3% on revenue +9.0% in FY26 as gross margin rose from 25.34% to 28.91%. Offsets: finance cost +88.5% to Rs 117.60 m on higher borrowings, contract labour +52.4% to Rs 212.53 m, and a Rs 74.96 m FX loss against Rs 1.24 m (pp. 443-444). Other income of Rs 26.31 m includes grants and credit-loss write-backs.
- **Operating leverage.** EBITDA margin improved from 6.52% (FY24) to 10.89% (FY26) as fixed costs were spread; adjusted for FX, FY26 was 12.32% (p. 182).

- **Drivers.** Revenue growth Bear 5% / 5% / 5% / 4% / 4%, Neutral 16% / 16% / 14% / 12% / 10%, Bull 27% / 24% / 20% / 15% / 11%; reported-EBITDA margin 9.5% / 11.5% / 13.0%; working capital 31% / 27% / 23% of revenue; terminal ROIC 12% / 17% / 22%. Rationale and cites are in the workbook. Bear excludes the capex object and its funding. Capex per rupee of added revenue is measured over FY25-FY26 (0.133), not FY26 alone (0.65), because FY26 was the expansion year with only 9.0% growth; IPO capex falls in model year 2, in line with the issuer's FY28-FY29 schedule.
- **Relative.** Peer P/Es used: PG Electroplast 76.58x, Amber 141.15x, KRN 117.58x, Elin 19.08x (median 97.1x); EPack (526x on EPS of Rs 0.34) and Centum (loss) are excluded. The peer set is the DRHP's and is not comparable in size or model; we apply a 50% discount to the peer median. Peer data are per share from the DRHP (p. 179), not exchange quotes.
- **Divergence.** DCF and relative differ by about 77% in the Neutral case. The relative method prices FY26 earnings of Rs 33.21 cr at the multiple of listed ODMs, while the DCF prices cash: working capital absorbs 27% of each rupee of added revenue and free cash flow is negative in the first two forecast years. The gap is the investment question: whether earnings convert to cash.
- **Terminal-value guard.** The Neutral DCF breaches our 100% guard (terminal value 108% of enterprise value) because explicit-period free cash flow is negative; the Bull case sits at 95%. We show the Neutral figure but do not rely on it, and the blended Neutral value inherits that caveat. We did not change assumptions to pass the guard: the issuer's own plan holds working capital near 29% of revenue.
- **Reverse DCF.** Current free cash flow is negative, so no multiple can be stated, and the entire price at the reference rests on a turn in cash conversion that has not yet begun.
- **Post-money dilution.** If the Rs 3,000 m fresh issue were counted in full, FY26 PAT of Rs 332.06 m on equity of Rs 3,941.17 m would be a return of at most 8.4%, against the 42.86% shown pre-issue. At the reference price the pre-money P/E is 19.08x, EV/EBITDA about 13.6x (our arithmetic: market value Rs 6,338 m plus net debt Rs 1,442 m over reported EBITDA Rs 573.88 m) and P/B 6.7x on NAV of Rs 17.20. Promoter weighted average costs are Rs 1.81 to Rs 3.36 per share (cover page).
- The reference sits above all three blended values. That is a statement about the reference, which is ours; the actual band may differ materially.

**Supply overhang (at the reference price).** Promoter minimum contribution about 161.3 lakh shares locked for three years; promoter holding above it about 292.0 lakh shares for one year; other pre-issue holders about 63.5 lakh shares for six months (p. 121). Public float at listing is about 289.5 lakh shares (fresh 259.0 + OFS 30.5 lakh), so the six-month release (63.5 lakh) is 22% of float and the one-year release (292.0 lakh) is about the size of the float. Anchor allotments are discretionary and locked in (half for 30 days, half for 90 days), so cannot be sold on listing day.

## What would change our view

Measurable against the RHP:
1. H1 FY27 financials show operating cash flow above 50% of PAT and inventory days below 100.
2. The customs show-cause matter is resolved, or the exposure (duty, interest and penalty) is quantified at under 10% of net worth.
3. Purchases from Amber Enterprises are disclosed with terms, and Supplier 1 is identified; the top-10 customer share is below 85% in H1 FY27.
4. Orders are placed for at least half of the Rs 1,384.73 m for the new units, and the RHP gives the added capacity in units and the revenue the issuer expects from it.
5. The RHP states the revenue projection behind the working-capital plan and it is within our Neutral-to-Bull range, or the price band is at or below our blended values (Rs 59.25 / 76.33 / 92.98).
6. A hedging policy is disclosed and the FX loss in H1 FY27 is below 5% of PBT.
7. Copper and brass pass-through terms are disclosed for the top four customers.
In the other direction: a price band above Rs 115.82 with the same financials, or a larger share of revenue from Amber.

## Verification

- **Restated statements reproduce.** Expenses sum to the printed totals in all three years (FY26: 4,260.29 - 515.61 + 256.01 + 117.60 + 40.11 + 692.88 = 4,851.28); PBT = income less expenses (5,293.76 - 4,851.28 = 442.48); PAT = PBT less tax Rs 110.42 m = Rs 332.06 m. Total assets Rs 3,883.42 m equal total equity and liabilities in every year.
- **KPI EBITDA** reproduces: FY26 442.48 + 117.60 + 40.11 - 26.31 = 573.88; FY25 458.91; FY24 156.35. Operating profit for eligibility reproduces (533.77 / 429.41 / 135.94; average 366.37).
- **Cash flow.** FY26 net movement -430.08 - 302.97 + 732.93 = -0.12, as printed.
- **EPS.** Rs 332.06 m / 54,729,240 shares = Rs 6.07; the FY24 figure uses about 53.6 m shares because of a share issue in FY24 (Rs 1.43).
- **Issuer working-capital table** reproduces the balance sheet: inventory 1,700.24 + receivables 1,061.51 + other current assets 13.73 + 115.64 - payables 1,180.04 - 54.02 - 24.58 = 1,632.48.
- **No document inconsistency** was found in the statements; the note's "adjusted" EBITDA (Rs 648.84 m) is the issuer's own and is not used in the model.
- **Not tested:** the investor presentation was not reviewed; the industry report was not read in full; the image-only annexure check was not needed because the text layer was readable.
- **Outside sources** are used only in the merchant-banker section and are marked unverified.
- **Workbooks:** the toolkit verification result is recorded in the pull request.
