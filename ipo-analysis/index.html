How the desk works

  1. DetectTwice a day the desk scans the NSE issue calendar and SEBI's register of red herring prospectuses. An issue enters coverage when its RHP is filed and its price band is announced. Draft prospectuses are not covered.
  2. AnalyseThe RHP is read end to end: business, industry, moat and SWOT, management and governance, three years of financials, an earnings-quality test, projections, a DCF and peer comparison, and the anchor book.
  3. VerifyEvery model is rebuilt from the offer document and checked by an automated gate (49 tests) before anything is published. Every number carries a page reference.
  4. PublishThe report, PDF and editable Excel models go live here automatically, before the issue opens for subscription.

The call, judged at the upper band of the price band

SubscribeNeutral fair value within 10% of the price, or above it.
NeutralNeutral fair value 10-30% below the price, or the Bull case at or above the price.
AvoidNeutral fair value more than 30% below the price and the Bull case also below it, or a material governance or accounting issue.

Fair value blends a DCF (house cost of capital, three scenarios) with peer-median multiples 50:50. Normal features of a business are context, not deductions. Grey-market premium is never used.
